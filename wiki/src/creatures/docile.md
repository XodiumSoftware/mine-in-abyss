# Docile

Calm creatures that leave you alone — until you give them a reason not to. Several are
substantially tougher than the hostile creatures of the same layer.

<div class="recipe-table">

| Creature | Base | Hearts | Layers |
| :------- | :--- | -----: | :----- |
| **Beniguma** | Wolf | 16 | First, Second |
| **Chimokami** | Fox | 30 | First |
| **Daikonchu** | Silverfish | 80 | Third |
| **Hisoishi** | Endermite | 25 | Second, Third |
| **Rinsipede** | Silverfish | 30 | Third |
| **Rockwalker** | Silverfish | 25 | Second, Third |
| **Spikewalker** | Silverfish | 40 | Fifth |
| **Titanjaw** | Turtle | 60 | Fifth |

</div>

## Notes

- **Daikonchu is the outlier.** At 80 hearts it outclasses every hostile creature in the Great
  Fault, and it is Docile — leaving it alone is usually the right choice.
- **Four of the eight are built on a Silverfish**, which makes them small, fast, and hard to hit.
- **Chimokami** lives only in the First, and at 30 hearts is one of the toughest creatures that
  far up.
